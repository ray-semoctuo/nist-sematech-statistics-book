Discussion Note the absence of structure in the above lag plot. There is no associative ability to infer from a current value Y(i-1) as to what the next value Y(i) will be. Thus for a known value Y(i-1) on the horizontal axis (say, Y(i-1) = +0.5), the Y(i)-th value could be virtually anything (from Y(i) = -2.5 to Y(i) = +1.5). Such non-associativity is the essense of randomness. In short, adjacent abservations do not "co-relate", we thus speak of this case as "no autocorrelation".
