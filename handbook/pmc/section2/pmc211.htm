6.2.1. How do we Monitor Product for acceptability (Lot Acceptance Sampling)?

6.2.1.1.

Philosophy
Difference between acceptance sampling and quality control Harold Dodge is considered by many to be the father of acceptance sampling as a statistical science. He indicated that there is a philosophical as well as practical difference between acceptance sampling plans and acceptance quality control. 

Acceptance sampling is execution of a specific sampling plan. It does NOT perform quality control. The principle of a sampling plan is based on probability theory. The user of a sampling plan selects n observations at random from a lot and tests them. If at least a certain number of defects occur, the lot is rejected.  The number of defects is denoted by the letter c and the sampling plan is denoted by (n,c). The testing is performed without replacement. 

The underlying distribution on which sampling plans are based is the hypergometric distribution. If there is no software available, this distribution is sometimes approximated by the binomial distribution which is easier to compute than the hypergeometric. Strictly speaking, the binomial distribution is used for sampling with replacement.

On the other hand, acceptance quality control is much broader in scope, involving quality control charts, capability indices and process improvement using design of experiments
