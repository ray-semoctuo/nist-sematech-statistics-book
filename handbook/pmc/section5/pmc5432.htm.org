<td VALIGN=TOP WIDTH="15%"><!-- Add marginal notes below -->
<I>
Computation of the T<sup> 2</sup> statistic
</I>
</td>

<td VALIGN=TOP WIDTH="85%">
The sample set consists of n = 13 observations, so
<p>
<img SRC="eqns/mvar17.gif" height=222 width=446 ALT=
"T(0)**2 = 13*(Xbar-0)*S**(-1)*(Xbar-0
 = 13*[-1.077  0.615  0.923]*[0.9952  0.0258  -0.3867;
 0.0258  1.3271  0.0936; -0.3867  0.0936  2.5959]*[-1.077  0.615  0.923]
 = 13*[-1.400  0.876  2.87][-1.077  0.615  0.923] = 60.99">
<p>
The corresponding <i>F</i> value =
(T<sub>0</sub><sup>2</sup>)(<i>n-p)/[p(n-1)]</i>=60.99(10)/3(12)
=16.94.
</td>
</tr>

<!-- begin paragraph -->
<TR>
<TD WIDTH=15% VALIGN=top>
<!-- Add marginal notes below -->
<i>We reject that the sample met the target mean</i>
</TD>
<TD VALIGN=TOP WIDTH=85%>
<!-- Add main text below -->
The <i>p</i>-value at 16.94 for <i>F</i> <sub>3,10</sub> = .00030.
Since this is considerably smaller than an
<img align=absmiddle src="eqns/alpha.gif" alt="alpha"> of, say,
.01, we must reject <i>H</i><sub>0</sub> at least a 1% level of
significance.  Therefore, we conclude that, on the average, the
sample did not meet the targets for the means.
