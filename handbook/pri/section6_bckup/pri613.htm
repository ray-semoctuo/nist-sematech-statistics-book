Interaction Effects
Check for Interaction Effects: Dex Mean Interaction Plot In addition to the main effects, it is also important to check for interaction effects, especially 2-term interaction effects. The dex mean interaction effects plot is an effective tool for this.

The three plots on the diagonal below are a replotting of the main effects from the prior dex mean plot; they are plots of the average response versus each of the three factors. The off-diagonal plots display the average response (vertically) versus the interaction (here = the cross-product) horizontally. These interaction plots are possible because of the +1, -1 scaling that was chosen for the individual factors. In such case when the individual factors are +1 and -1, then the cross-product X1*X2 also takes on values of +1 and -1, and so the interactions may be treated, plotted, interpreted, and compared in exactly the same fashion as the main effects. Thus for the middle plot on the top, the horizontal axis is X1*X2, where X1 takes on values -1 and +1, X2 takes on -1 and +1, and thus X1*X2 takes on values -1 and +1. Comparing the average response at these 2 levels, it is seen that they are near-identical and so the X1*X2 interaction effect is near zero (-0.1275 to be exact).

The legend within each plot is the least squares estimate of the factor (or interaction) effect. Note that in this 2-level orthogonal design case, the least squares effect estimates remarkably degenerate to a simple difference of the 2 mean values, high and low.

Conclusions from the DEX Interaction Effects Plot We make the following conclusions from the dex interaction effects plot:
  1. Ranking of Factors: Looking for the plots which have steepest lines (that is, largest effects), we note that:

    • X1 (number of turns) is the most important effect: estimated effect = -3.1025;
    • X2 (winding distance) is the next most important factor: estimated effect = -0.8675;
    • X3 (wire guage) is relatively unimportant; estimated effect = -0.2125;
    • All three 2-term interactions are relatively unimporant; estimated effects all less than 0.30 in magnitude.

  2. Best Settings: As with the main effects plot, the best settings to maximize the (average) sensitivity are

      (X1,X2,X3) = (+1,-1,+1)

    but with the X3 setting of +1 mattering little.
