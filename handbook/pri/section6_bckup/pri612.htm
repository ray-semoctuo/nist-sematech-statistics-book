5.6.1.2.

Main Effects
Plot the Data: Dex Scatter Plot A first step in the analysis is to plot the data. The dex scatter plot is an effective tool for this.

Conclusions from the DEX Scatter Plot We can make the following conclusions based on the dex scatter plot.
  1. Important Factors: Factor 1 (Number of Turns) is clearly important, since when X1 = -1, all 4 senstivities are low (all less than 2) and when X1 = +1, all 4 sensitivities are high (all higher than 3). Factor 2 (Winding Distance) is less important, since the 4 sensitivities for X2 = -1 are only slightly higher than the 4 sensitivities for X2 = +1. Factor 3 (Wire Guage) does not appear to be important at all, since the sensitivity is about the same (on the average) regardless of the settings for X3.

  2. Best Settings: The best settings depend on the definition of "best" for the response. In this experiment, we are using the device as a detector, and so high sensitivities are desirable. Given this, our first pass at best settings yields (X1 = +1, X2 = -1, X3 = either).
Check for Main Effects: Dex Mean Plot One of the primary questions is what are the most important factors? One aspect of this is to identify any significant main effects. The dex mean plot is an effective tool for this.

Conclusions from the DEX Mean Plot The dex mean plot (or main effects plot) reaffirms the ordering of the dex scatter plot, but additional information is gleaned because the eyeball distance between the mean values gives an approximation to the least squares estimate for the factor effects.

We make the following conclusions from the above dex mean plot:

  1. Ranking of Factors:
    X1 (effect = large : about 3 ohms)
    X2 (effect = moderate: about -1 ohm)
    X3 (effect = small : about 1/4 ohm)

  2. Best Settings: As before, choose those factor settings which (on the average) maximize the sensitivity, namely,

      (X1,X2,X3) = (+,-,+)
Comparison of Plots Both the dex scatter plot and the dex mean plot are useful first steps in viewing the data from a factorial experiment. The dex mean plot shows the main effects more clearly than the dex scatter plot. However, the dex scatter plot will show more detail, such as the presence of outliers, that will be hidden in the dex mean plot. We recommend generating both plots in the initial look at the data.
