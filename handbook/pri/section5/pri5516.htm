Single response: Optimization subject to experimental region constraints
Ridge analysis is a method of finding optimal factor settings that satisfy certain constraints  Sometimes the optimal operating conditions  simply fall outside the region where the experiment was conducted. In these cases constrained optimization techniques can be used to find the solution  that optimizes  without leaving the region in the factor space where the experiment took place.
